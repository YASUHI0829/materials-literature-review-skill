# Review Outline

## 1. Research Objective

Define the target: predict selected mechanical-property outputs from microstructure or texture-derived inputs.

## 2. Material Background

Summarize direct target-material evidence. Separate processing, microstructure, texture, formability, and property literature.

## 3. Physics Model Baseline

Cover constitutive laws, material-specific mechanisms, and virtual material tests. Identify which papers support model choice and which support parameter ranges.

## 4. Calibration and Optimization

Review Bayesian optimization, Bayesian calibration, surrogate-assisted search, sensitivity analysis, and uncertainty quantification.

## 5. Machine Learning Evolution

Review texture-to-property, microstructure-to-property, physics-model surrogate, and physics-informed ML papers. Identify input representation and label strategy.

## 6. Integrated Workflow

Explain how measured microstructure can become features, how physics simulations provide labels or augmentation, and how experiments anchor validation.

## 7. Gap and Contribution

State what remains missing in the literature and how the proposed work fills it.
